01 / ABOUT

A systems view of engineering.

My technical interests span the full signal chain: from RF front ends and high-speed analog circuits to FPGA-based processing, embedded control, and measurement automation.

I hold a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from BYU–Idaho and am pursuing an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Johns Hopkins University. I’m especially interested in the connection between careful hardware design, rigorous measurement, and useful software tools.
